# Supplementary Methods, Figures, Tables, and Data

Companion to *The reproducible proximal-tubule→receiver communication landscape in human acute kidney injury is soluble- and extracellular-matrix-dominated, not extracellular-vesicle-specific: a pre-registered, phase-gated specificity test* (`manuscript\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\_ev\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\_aki\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\_ckd.md`). All content derives from frozen repository artifacts (fixed random seed 1); no analysis is re-run here. Every supplementary data table listed in §S3 is provided as a machine-readable file in `supplementary\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\_data/`, and every supplementary figure in §S2 is provided as a 600-dpi PNG plus an editable SVG in `supplementary\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\_figures/`, so the underlying values behind each gate decision are directly auditable.

## S1. Complete step-by-step methodology

This section states the full executed protocol, step by step, in execution order. It restates and expands the main-text Methods (§2.1–2.7) with the operational detail needed to reproduce every table and figure from the frozen artifacts. All thresholds, gene sets, and gate criteria were fixed before the corresponding analysis; a single random seed (`SEED = 1`) was used throughout.

### S1.1 Design, pre-registration, and claim ceiling

1. The study was pre-registered as a five-pillar, phase-gated program; four phases were executed. The registered spatial-adjacency (Pillar 3), genetic-enrichment (Pillar 4: GWAS/eQTL/pQTL), and urinary-EV detection and repurposing (Pillar 5) components were registered to run only after Gate-2 passage; Gate 2 did not pass, so those pillars were never triggered, and no result here depends on them. The executed Phases 3–4 instantiate the pre-registered EV-specificity-test component as a route-agnostic landscape with a constructive subtraction. A registered-versus-executed accounting is given in main-text §2.1 (Table 0A).
2. Each executed phase had a gate with criteria and numeric thresholds fixed before analysis (Gate 1: §S1.4 step 8; Gate 2: §S1.5 step 9; Gate 3: §S1.6 step 9; Gate 4: §S1.7 step 6). Verdict ledgers are Tables S3, S12, S27, S39.
3. A fixed language ceiling was part of the pre-registration: all findings are discovery-grade associations; "communication" denotes inferred co-expression candidacy rather than measured flux; no claim of EV cargo transfer is made. Transcriptomics can only observe machinery *transcription*, not vesicle biogenesis, secretion, trafficking, uptake, or function.

### S1.2 Public data retrieval and freezing

**Bulk kidney transcriptomes (Phase 1).**

1. Ten public human bulk-transcriptome series were retrieved from the NCBI Gene Expression Omnibus (GEO): GSE139061, GSE30718, GSE142153, GSE53769, GSE104954, GSE47185, GSE99340, GSE294519, GSE104948, and GSE339456 (nine kidney-tissue series on RNA-seq and microarray platforms, plus GSE142153 profiling peripheral blood mononuclear cells), spanning native AKI, transplant acute tubular necrosis (ATN), CKD, diabetic kidney disease, and IgA nephropathy (Table S1).
2. Roles (discovery / replication / progression) and control types were recorded in a frozen cohort table (Table S1); each cohort was scored on five pre-registered criteria (kidney tissue confirmed; sample-level phenotype clarity; appropriate control group; platform quality and adequate sample size; clinical metadata availability) and assigned a confidence tier. Tier A (score 5, no caveat) is the primary, gate-governing analysis; Tier B covers pre-specified sensitivity analyses only; Tier C is exploratory. A phase-0 data-availability audit preceded execution (Tables S2, S54).
3. Three cohorts were executed in Phase 1: native AKI GSE139061 (39 AKI native biopsies vs 9 nephrectomy references; RNA-seq, quantile-normalized deposit) and transplant ATN GSE30718 (28 ATN vs 19 controls; Affymetrix GPL570) and GSE53769 (8 ATN vs 10 protocol-biopsy controls; Affymetrix GPL16686 (HuGene-2.0-ST) microarray; 18 zero-hour/pre-implantation biopsies excluded as a distinct baseline). Cohorts were analyzed as separate contrasts and never pooled into a single meta-analysis across injury contexts.
4. Deposit validity was checked before analysis: the GSE139061 processed matrix was verified quantile-normalized (log2 transform correct; all column sums ≈ 8.06×10⁶), and sample labels were matched to GEO metadata.

**Single-cell/single-nucleus kidney atlases (Phases 2–3).**

1. Cell-level expression was queried from the CZ CELLxGENE Census, version `2025-01-30` (*Homo sapiens*) via the `cellxgene-census` API, restricted at pull time to `tissue\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\_general == "kidney"`, `is\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\_primary\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\_data == True`, `assay == "10x 3' v3"`, and `disease ∈ {normal, acute kidney failure, chronic kidney disease}`.
2. This returned seven datasets (samples: normal 587, CKD 544, AKI 238). Two large multi-disease atlases carry the within-atlas disease contrasts used throughout: `a12ccb9b-4fbe-457d-8590-ac78053259ef` (single-nucleus; 16 AKI donors) and `dea717d4-7bc0-4e46-950f-fd7e1cc8df7d` (single-cell; 14 AKI donors). Both are assay arms of one atlas collection (Lake, Menon, Winfree et al., main-text ref. 17; Census collection `0f528c8a-a25c-4840-8fa3-d156fa11086f`) and share 34 donors overall (10 of 16 snRNA and 10 of 14 scRNA AKI donors; 19 CKD; 5 normal). Cross-atlas agreement is therefore cross-assay, partially same-donor replication, not independent-cohort replication; a shared-donor exclusion sensitivity (Table S77) and an external replication cohort (Table S78) accompany the primary result. Phase 2 fits a pooled multi-dataset pseudobulk model (dataset terms plus donor-level correlation; §S1.5); Phase 3 runs engines per arm and requires both to pass (§S1.6).
3. External replication cohort: GSE210622 (main-text ref. 19), an independent single-nucleus AKI study (4 non-COVID post-mortem AKI donors; 6 controls: 3 tumor-nephrectomy adjacent, 3 post-mortem), 10x 3′ v3, author cell-type labels mapped onto the frozen compartments; the deposit's integrated COVID-19 AKI arm was excluded.

### S1.3 Frozen EV gene-set ladder, controls, and injury panel (all phases)

1. A frozen EV gene-set ladder (version v1.3) was assembled from Gene Ontology terms via QuickGO (exosome `GO:0070062`; multivesicular-body/biogenesis `GO:0005771`, `GO:0036258`), UniProt secreted-protein annotation, and curated biogenesis/machinery lists.
2. Ladder keys and sizes (Table S49): `EV\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\_TIER1\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\_PRIMARY` (152), `EV\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\_TIER1\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\_MACHINERY` (73), `EV\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\_MVB\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\_PATHWAY` (114), `EV\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\_TIER2\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\_CARGO` (2115), `NONEV\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\_SECRETED\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\_BACKGROUND` (2097), `CTRL\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\_MITOCHONDRIAL` (1668), `CTRL\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\_RIBOSOMAL` (304), `CTRL\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\_LYSO\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\_ENDOSOMAL` (2381).
3. A frozen 38-cytokine soluble negative-control panel (Table S50) gates the route-attribution rule in Phases 3–4. EV-cargo definitions follow the MISEV2023 reporting standard (main-text ref. 18). The pre-registration planned ExoCarta/Vesiclepedia as the cargo source; direct downloads were access-blocked, and the cargo universe was built instead from GO/QuickGO, MSigDB exosome sets, and a curated biogenesis list (logged as pre-registration contingency C-new; Tables S2, S54). The full membership of every frozen set is provided as machine-readable files (Tables S80–S89).
4. A frozen tubular-injury severity panel (HAVCR1, LCN2, SPP1, TIMP1, KRT20, VCAM1, CST3, PROM1) was curated with zero overlap with `EV\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\_TIER1\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\_PRIMARY`; no clinical severity scores exist in any deposit, so this transcriptomic proxy is used throughout. Two panel members carry dual roles and are disclosed: SPP1 is also the headline communication ligand of Phase 3 (discussed in main-text §4), and KRT20 is primarily a urothelial marker, retained as frozen at registration; the property the severity control relies on is the panel's zero overlap with the EV Tier-1 set.

### S1.4 Phase 1: bulk-transcriptome EV-program test (Gate 1), step-by-step

1. **Per-cohort differential expression.** limma (`trend=TRUE, robust=TRUE`), design `\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\~ group`; probe→gene collapse by highest-mean-expression probe; effect = log2 fold-change, variance = (moderated SE)². GSE53769 (Affymetrix HuGene-2.0-ST) was mapped from the GPL16686 platform annotation to gene symbols via GB\_ACC/RefSeq accessions (org.Hs.eg.db). Full per-cohort outputs: Tables S51–S53.
2. **Transplant random-effects meta-analysis (Analysis B).** Per-gene random-effects meta-analysis (metafor; DerSimonian-Laird primary, REML sensitivity) across the two transplant cohorts on 12,110 shared genes (Table S11).
3. **Enrichment (primary test).** GSEA on genome-wide ranked log2 fold-change over the frozen ladder (primary, gate-governing), plus hypergeometric over-representation analysis (ORA) over differentially expressed genes (secondary). Native: Tables S4–S5; transplant: Tables S6–S7.
4. **EV-specificity permutation.** Covariate-matched permutation (B = 10,000) comparing each set's signed rank-score to random non-EV sets matched on per-gene expression level (limma AveExpr); one-sided empirical p for enrichment above background (Table S8). Gene-length matching was omitted (annotation unavailable); expression level captures the dominant DE-detectability confound.
5. **Cross-context concordance (Analysis C).** Genome-wide Spearman correlation of native vs transplant-meta log2 fold-changes (11,205 shared genes), a binomial sign test, and an EV-core-restricted repeat (Table S9).
6. **Severity analysis (Analysis D, exploratory).** Per-sample ssGSEA (gseapy) of EV Tier-1 vs the frozen injury panel; Spearman correlation across all samples and within cases, per context (Table S10).
7. **Specificity verdict.** The ribosomal set, a generic translational-response comparator pre-registered as a required-null control, was required to be null in both contexts; it was enriched in both, so the ladder is not EV-clean at the bulk level under the pre-registered rule. Because ribosomal induction is expected injury biology, we additionally report the comparison quantitatively (permutation z-scores; main-text §3.1) rather than as a binary violation alone.
8. **Gate 1 evaluation** against the pre-registered criteria: 1A native EV enrichment (FDR < 0.05 and NES > 0); 1B transplant EV enrichment; 1C-i cross-context concordance (p < 0.05); 1C-ii enrichment above matched background in both contexts; 1C-iii clean ribosomal negative control. Ledger: Table S3. **Outcome: FAIL** (1B only passes, qualified).

### S1.5 Phase 2: single-cell/pseudobulk EV-program test (Gate 2), step-by-step

1. **Pseudobulk construction.** Raw integer counts were summed per donor × compartment × suspension sample: 1,369 samples × 61,888 genes. Floors: ≥25 cells per sample; ≥10 donors per disease for a "primary" compartment.
2. **Frozen compartments.** PT (primary sender = epithelial cell of proximal tubule); tubular\_other; receiver\_endothelial; receiver\_myeloid; receiver\_stromal; injury\_context (podocyte, parietal epithelial cell). All six clear the ≥10-donor primary floor in AKI, CKD, and normal.
3. **Differential expression (primary engine).** limma-voom with duplicate-correlation on donor. The model is fitted on the pooled multi-dataset pseudobulk (1,369 samples; six datasets contribute PT-level samples after cell floors), in which 34 donors are assayed by both atlas chemistries (10 of 16 snRNA and 10 of 14 scRNA AKI donors; 19 CKD; 5 normal), so the donor-level correlation is estimable and required; final model `\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\~ dataset\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\_id + disease\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\_code` (each dataset is single-assay, so suspension chemistry is perfectly nested in dataset\_id and absorbed by its coefficient).
4. **Sensitivity engine.** edgeR-QLF on donor-collapsed counts; voom↔edgeR log2FC concordance r ≥ 0.83 in every contrast (PT AKI-vs-normal r = 0.946).
5. **Contrast hierarchy (locked).** PRIMARY: AKI vs normal (falsifiable induction test). SECONDARY: CKD vs normal. EXPLORATORY: AKI vs CKD.
6. **Enrichment and specificity.** The frozen Pillar-1 ladder and enrichment engine (GSEA prerank + ORA + covariate-matched permutation, B = 10,000) applied to the injured-PT sender on the primary contrast (Tables S13–S16). Rankings indexed by gene symbol (feature\_id→symbol via a frozen gene map; duplicate symbols collapsed by max |log2FC|).
7. **Supporting (non-gating) analyses.** (i) PT abundance per donor (Mann-Whitney, Cliff's δ; Tables S21, S26); (ii) per-donor PT EV Tier-1 module score (donor-collapsed CPM, log1p, z across donors, mean over the set; Tables S22, S24); (iii) severity-within-PT: Spearman of the module score against the injury-panel score (Tables S23, S25); (iv) descriptive receiver-compartment GSEA (Table S20).
8. **Robustness.** Per-dataset DE in each shared atlas (Table S17), leave-one-dataset-out (Table S18), ≥2-dataset same-direction check, and a balanced 24-vs-24 down-sampled-control sensitivity (1,000 resamples, edgeR-QLF; Table S19).
9. **Gate 2 evaluation** against the pre-registered criteria: 2A PT EV enrichment (NES > 0 and FDR < 0.05) plus ≥1 independent-context replication; 2B enrichment above matched background (p < 0.05) with a clean ribosomal control; effect-size floor (|NES| > 1 and median EV log2FC ≥ 0.20); 2C technical robustness; 2D biological replication. Ledger: Table S12. **Outcome: FAIL** (2A, 2B, and the floor all fail; the floor outcome is engine-sensitive (it fails on the pre-registered primary engine and would pass on the sensitivity engine), but the 2A/2B failures are engine-robust and independently sufficient).

### S1.6 Phase 3: route-agnostic ligand-receptor landscape (Gate 3), step-by-step

1. **Shards.** Per (dataset × condition), cells were normalized and subsampled to a fixed cap per compartment (primary 2,000; sensitivity 4,000; seed 1). The smallest compartment in the study is dea717d4 stromal-AKI at 790 cells, flagged transparently throughout.
2. **Two independently implemented engines on identical cells.** CellChat v2 (R, v2.2.0; CellChatDB v2; probability + permutation p-value per interaction, B = 100 permutations, so p is resolved to p < 0.01) and LIANA rank\_aggregate (Python, v1.10.0; consensus of multiple LR methods; magnitude\_rank is a normalized robust-rank-aggregation score in \[0,1], lower = stronger). Raw per-condition engine outputs: Tables S55–S66 (CellChat LR and pathway level) and S67–S72 (LIANA). Full software versions: Table S79.
3. **Axis keying.** Each axis is keyed order-invariantly as `receiver | canon(ligand) | canon(receptor)`; sender = PT; receivers = myeloid, stromal, endothelial (frozen Cell Ontology term sets).
4. **A-priori absolute thresholds (fixed before inference).** CellChat significance p < 0.05; CellChat effect floor Δprob(AKI−normal) ≥ 0.05 or relative increase ≥ 20%. LIANA floor: rank-improvement ≥ 50 or RRA-improvement ≥ 0.15; LIANA direction (up-in-AKI) requires both rank and RRA to improve.
5. **Consensus logic.** Direction concordance required in both engines; statistical support uses OR-logic (significant in ≥1 engine, as fixed in the pre-registration); a per-dataset pass requires direction-both and significant-≥1 and floor-both; a replicated consensus requires passing in both atlas arms. The stricter AND-rule sensitivity (significant in both engines) leaves the funnel unchanged from S3 onward (Table S74). Per-engine differential tables: Tables S30–S33; per-axis per-atlas effects: Tables S29 (cap 2000) and S38 (cap 4000); consensus landscapes: Tables S28 and S37.
6. **Specificity controls (Gate 3B).** Scrambled-cell-label negative control (2 seeds; the consensus must collapse); pathway coherence (the consensus pathways must rank among the top up-in-AKI PT pathways; Table S34).
7. **Robustness controls (Gate 3C).** Leave-one-donor-out across all AKI donors (16 in a12ccb9b, 14 in dea717d4); subsample-cap sensitivity re-analysis of the full pipeline at cap 4,000, tiering axes by whether they re-clear the floor in both atlases (Tier 1 cap-robust vs Tier 2 magnitude-borderline). Consolidated evidence: Table S48; per-axis dossier: Table S42.
8. **Biological coherence (Gate 3D).** The identical consensus logic applied to CKD-vs-normal to test which acute axes persist into the fibrotic state (Tables S35–S36); PT-composition control from Pillar 2 (no PT-fraction shift, p = 0.19) excludes a compositional artifact.
9. **EV-route overlay (frozen, descriptive only).** Each consensus ligand annotated by the frozen route-attribution rule (§S1.8); per the pre-registration this overlay never enters the Gate 3 verdict. **Gate 3 outcome: FULL PASS** (ledger: Table S27).

### S1.7 Phase 4: constructive synthesis and EV subtraction (Gate 4), step-by-step

1. **No new inference.** Phase 4 reused the frozen Phase-3 artifacts (per-axis per-dataset evidence table, consensus landscape, EV route overlay, and the 18 raw engine tables).
2. **Strictly nested evidence-refinement funnel.** For every unique PT→receiver axis: S1 = candidate (any engine, any dataset); S2 = direction-concordant in both engines in ≥1 atlas; S3 = additionally significant in ≥1 engine; S4 = additionally above the a-priori effect-size floor (a full single-atlas pass = "reproducible"); S5 = passing the full chain in both atlases ("consensus"). Each stage is a verified strict subset of the previous. Master provenance: Table S40.
3. **Expression-floor sensitivity.** A cross-engine expression proxy floor was swept before the funnel (no floor; drop bottom 10%, 25%, 50%) to test whether the starting count and collapse shape were artifacts of low-expression candidates (Table S44; Figure S1).
4. **Route attribution and subtraction.** The frozen rule (§S1.8) was applied to every survivor: whole-landscape subtraction accounting (Table S43), consensus route partition (Table S45), reproducible-survivor routes (Table S46), and the supporting landscape (n = 41: direction-concordant and significant in ≥1 atlas, not consensus; Table S47).
5. **Cross-pillar specificity ledger.** The EV signal vs matched generic control from each pillar was consolidated into one ledger (Table S41).
6. **Gate 4 evaluation** (synthesis-integrity gate, not a hypothesis-rejection gate): 4A faithful subtraction (frozen rule + 0/38 soluble gate + no post-hoc reclassification); 4B clean primary/secondary separation with mandated naming; 4C quantitative dominance of soluble+ECM over EV-exclusive survivors; 4D consistency with the locked Gate 1/2/3 verdict files. Ledger: Table S39. **Outcome: FULL PASS.**

### S1.8 Frozen route-attribution rule

Each axis's ligand was tagged **EV-exclusive** (transcriptomic definition) if and only if it was in `EV\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\_TIER2\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\_CARGO` and not in `NONEV\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\_SECRETED\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\_BACKGROUND`; otherwise it was assigned by the CellChat `annotation` field (Secreted Signaling → **soluble-classical**, ECM-Receptor → **ECM**, Cell-Cell Contact → **contact**); any ligand lacking both frozen membership and an annotation was reported as **unclassified**. The EV rule was evaluated first. Both memberships are curated-list properties, not sequence inferences: the background is the UniProt secreted-protein annotation, which also covers non-classically secreted proteins lacking a signal peptide (for example MIF), so background membership must not be read as "has a classical signal peptide." The rule tests EV-exclusivity, not EV routing. Operating characteristics: the negative control flagged 0/38 soluble cytokines (Table S50), and the positive control flagged 12/12 canonical signal-peptide-free EV cargo proteins (SDCBP, PDCD6IP, TSG101, HSPA8, ANXA2, GAPDH, HSP90AA1, CD9, CD81, CD63, FLOT1, FLOT2); 1,653 of 2,115 cargo-list members and 40 of 217 LR-universe ligands are flaggable in principle (Table S73). Terminology note: frozen data artifacts written before this revision use the original label "EV-compatible"; the construct is identical and is reported as "EV-exclusive" throughout this revision.
