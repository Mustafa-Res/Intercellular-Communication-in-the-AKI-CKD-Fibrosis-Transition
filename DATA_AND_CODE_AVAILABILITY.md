All data in this study are publicly available. Human bulk kidney transcriptomes come from the NCBI Gene Expression Omnibus (GEO) under accessions GSE139061, GSE30718, GSE142153, GSE53769, GSE104954, GSE47185, GSE99340, GSE294519, GSE104948, and GSE339456 (platforms GPL20301, GPL570, GPL16686, and ERCB microarray/FFPE RNA-seq). Human kidney single-cell and single-nucleus RNA-seq come from the CZ CELLxGENE Census (version 2025-01-30) via the cellxgene-census API, restricted to primary kidney data assayed with 10x 3′ v3 across normal, acute kidney failure, and chronic kidney disease; the two multi-disease atlases that carry the disease contrasts are Census datasets a12ccb9b-4fbe-457d-8590-ac78053259ef (single-nucleus) and dea717d4-7bc0-4e46-950f-fd7e1cc8df7d (single-cell). Extracellular-vesicle gene sets are curated from Gene Ontology (QuickGO; GO:0070062, GO:0005771, GO:0036258), UniProt secreted-protein annotation, and curated biogenesis lists, and are frozen as ev\_genesets\_frozen\_v1.3.json (SHA-256 046e780100d22906). Five open PRIDE ProteomeXchange urinary-EV proteome datasets (PXD050720, PXD065380, PXD042533, PXD009784, PXD025547) are identified for a planned urinary-EV proteomic cross-check. All analysis code (database access, differential expression, gene-set enrichment, ligand–receptor inference, and the EV-compatibility subtraction), together with the figure- and table-generating scripts, is available in the accompanying repository (00\_preregistration/code/ and the per-pillar 01\_-04\_/code/ folders); a row-per-deliverable traceability map is provided in the repository (TRACEABILITY\_results\_to\_source.csv). Analyses use a fixed random seed (1). All results are discovery-grade associations; no claim of EV-mediated cargo transfer is made.
